Sign in to My Closet

Add My Closet to your Home Screen first, then sign in there. A Home Screen app keeps its own sign-in, so signing in here in Safari wouldn't carry over.

  1. Tap the Share button.
  2. Choose Add to Home Screen.
  3. Open My Closet from your Home Screen and sign in.
